About Us

A Legacy of Faith,
Excellence & Purpose

Kingsdale Montessori School was established in 2008 with a burden to raise a generation of children who know God, love learning and live with purpose.

We are a Christian-based Montessori school committed to providing a child-centered education that develops the whole child – intellectually, spiritually, socially, emotionally and physically.

Using the Montessori standard, we train the 5 senses through practical life activities and sensorial materials that build concentration, independence and a deep love for learning.

Over the years, we have built a reputation for excellence, discipline, integrity and leadership, partnering with parents to raise children who influence their generation and transform society.

Our History

  • 2008

    Kingsdale Montessori School was founded with a vision to raise godly leaders through quality Montessori education.

  • 2012

    Became fully established as a Christian-based Montessori Institution with excellent facilities and qualified teachers.

  • 2016

    Joined Association of Christian Schools International (ACSI) and raised the standard of academic and moral excellence.

  • 2020

    Expanded our learning environment, introduced innovative learning tools and strengthened parent partnership.

  • Today

    We continue to nurture confident, creative and compassionate leaders who are impacting the world for Christ.
