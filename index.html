About Us

A School With Purpose

Kingsdale Montessori School is a Christian-based school committed to raising godly leaders who will make a positive impact in their world.

Using the Montessori approach, we provide a learning experience that builds curiosity, independence, discipline, and a love for lifelong learning.

We partner with parents to guide each child academically, socially and spiritually toward a bright future.
