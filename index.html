About Us

A School With Purpose

Kingsdale Montessori School was founded with a clear vision — to raise a generation of godly leaders who will excel academically, live with integrity and serve their communities with compassion. We combine the proven Montessori method with a strong Christian foundation.

Our classrooms are child-centred and carefully prepared, allowing each child to learn at their own pace, explore their interests and develop independence, concentration and confidence under the guidance of trained, loving educators.

We believe that educating a child is a partnership between school and home. Parents are warmly welcomed into the Kingsdale family as we work together to shape each child's future.
