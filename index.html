About Kingsdale

A school where children lead their own discovery.

Since 2014, Kingsdale Montessori has provided a prepared environment where children aged 18 months to 6 years develop independence, focus and a lifelong love of learning.

Our certified Montessori guides observe each child and tailor the curriculum to their unique pace, interests and developmental stage — blending practical life, sensorial, language, mathematics and cultural studies.

  • Authentic Montessori curriculum
  • Mixed-age classrooms (18m – 6yrs)
  • Prepared, child-sized environments
  • Focus on independence & concentration

Why Kingsdale

The Montessori difference

Everything we do is designed to honour the child — their pace, their interests and their potential.

  • 01

    Individualised Learning

    Every child works at their own pace with a personalised work plan.

  • 02

    Mixed-Age Classrooms

    Younger children learn from older peers; older children build leadership.

    Trained AMI Guides

    Prepared Environment
